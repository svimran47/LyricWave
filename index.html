Microphone Privacy & Audio Use

When you tap "Listen", LyricWave listens through your microphone for up to 12 seconds (most songs are found after 4) and turns the audio into an acoustic fingerprint on your device. Only that fingerprint is sent to the recognition service to identify the music playing nearby. If you turn on Auto re-listen, it takes a new sample about every 2 minutes while a microphone-identified song is showing.

  • Fingerprint only: With Shazam (the default) the audio itself never leaves your device; only a few-KB fingerprint of it does. ACRCloud and AudD, if you pick them in Settings, receive the audio sample.
  • Never Saved or Stored: Your recording is never saved to disk, logged, or retained after recognition.
  • Hardware Release: The microphone turns off as soon as each sample is captured, and whenever you cancel or switch source.

Fingerprints (or samples, for ACRCloud/AudD) are sent over HTTPS and matched by Shazam, ACRCloud, or AudD (selectable in Settings). See the Privacy Policy for details.
