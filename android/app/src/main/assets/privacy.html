Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 • Effective immediately

1. Overview

LyricWave ("we", "our", or "the app") provides real-time synchronized lyrics, acoustic music recognition, trending charts, and music news. This Privacy Policy discloses our data practices, in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations.

2. Microphone Audio & Acoustic Recognition

LyricWave uses your device's microphone only for song recognition, and only after you have accepted the in-app microphone notice. Recording starts when:

  • you tap "Tap to Listen", the microphone orb, "Listen again" or "Re-sync"; or
  • you have turned on Auto re-listen (off by default) while a song identified by the microphone is showing — LyricWave then takes a new sample about every 2 minutes to follow song changes; or
  • a song identified by the microphone finishes while the Microphone tab is open and the app is on screen, so the next song can be identified.

Switching to another source, cancelling, or leaving the Microphone tab stops recording and any pending re-listen.

  • Short samples: each listen lasts at most 12 seconds and stops as soon as the song is found; it is used only to identify the music playing.
  • Fingerprint on your device: the audio is converted into an acoustic fingerprint on your device. With Shazam (the default) only that fingerprint leaves the device — never the audio itself. ACRCloud and AudD, if you select them, receive the audio sample.
  • Desktop app: the desktop app sends the fingerprint directly to Shazam instead of through our endpoint.
  • Processors: the fingerprint (or sample) is sent over HTTPS to our recognition endpoint (hosted on Cloudflare) and matched via Shazam (Privacy Policy), ACRCloud (Privacy Policy) or AudD (Privacy Policy), depending on the engine selected in Settings.
  • No audio storage: LyricWave does not keep, log or sell audio samples. Your IP address is used briefly in memory to rate-limit recognition requests and is not stored.

2a. Music Playing on Your Phone (Android app, optional)

If you turn on Notification access for LyricWave, the Android app can follow the song playing in other apps (for example Spotify, YouTube Music or Apple Music) so lyrics stay in sync.

  • What is read: only the active media player's song title, artist, album, artwork link, song length, playback position and play/pause state, and which app is playing.
  • What is never read: the contents of your notifications — messages, codes, emails or anything else. LyricWave's notification listener ignores every notification.
  • Where it goes: this information stays on your device. Only the song title and artist (and length) are sent to LRCLIB to look up lyrics, the same as for any other source.
  • Controls: LyricWave's play, pause and seek buttons send those commands to the app you're listening with. You can turn Notification access off at any time in Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications (wording varies by phone).

2b. Beat Sync (Android app, optional)

The "Pulse" lyric animation can react to the beat and vocals of the music playing on your phone. To do this, LyricWave uses Android's audio visualizer to read the loudness levels (a frequency spectrum) of the audio output, about 20 times per second.

  • Android requires the microphone/audio permission for this, but the microphone is not used and no sound is recorded.
  • Only a handful of numbers (bass, vocal and overall levels, and beat timing) are calculated on your device to animate the lyrics. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
  • It only runs while the lyrics screen is open and the app is on screen. If you don't grant the permission, the animation follows the rhythm of the lyrics instead.

2c. Music Playing on Your Computer (desktop app)

The This PC tab of the desktop app reads the song title, artist, album and playback position that your operating system's media controls already show (Windows media sessions, Spotify/Music on macOS, MPRIS players on Linux). It does not record audio. Only the title and artist are sent to LRCLIB to find lyrics.

2d. projectM Visualizer Audio (optional)

By default the fullscreen projectM visualizer follows the song's beat without listening to anything. If you turn on projectM reacts to live audio, it analyses system audio (desktop app) or the microphone (elsewhere) frame by frame on your device to drive the visuals. This audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and capture stops when you leave fullscreen.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Spotify (optional): connects through Spotify's OAuth 2.0 PKCE flow and requests user-read-currently-playing, user-read-playback-state and user-read-private (used to show your display name and profile picture). Access and refresh tokens and your cached Spotify profile are stored in this app's local storage on your device; they are sent only to Spotify, never to LyricWave servers. You can disconnect in the app or revoke access in your Spotify account settings.
  • Last.fm (optional): the username you enter is sent to the Last.fm API to read your public "now playing" scrobbles and track lengths, and is stored on your device.
  • Lyrics, search and charts: song titles and artist names are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to fetch lyrics, and search terms to the Apple iTunes Search API for artwork and metadata. Top-chart and music-news lists are loaded from Apple's public charts feed and our Cloudflare endpoints.
  • Library: the songs you play are saved in a library on your device (IndexedDB). To match each song to open music data, its title, artist, length and ISRC code are sent to MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org), usually through our Cloudflare endpoint; opening an artist also loads their public description from Wikidata, their photo from Wikimedia Commons, and similar artists from ListenBrainz (labs.api.listenbrainz.org, sent only the artist's MusicBrainz id), and cover images load from the Cover Art Archive. Your library itself never leaves your device, and "Clear All Data" deletes it.
  • Free music: when you search Free music, your search words go to Openverse (api.openverse.org, run by WordPress.org). Songs you play stream straight from the site that hosts them (for example Jamendo or Wikimedia Commons), which sees your IP address like any website. Nothing is downloaded for offline use.
  • Backups: a library backup is a file you create and keep yourself. LyricWave never uploads it; restoring reads it on your device only.
  • Playlists: your playlists are saved only on your device (IndexedDB). "Clear All Data" deletes them.
  • Your lyrics: lyrics you add to a song, with the rights you chose for them, are saved only on your device (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded. "Clear All Data" deletes them.
  • Fonts: the app loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when fonts are downloaded.

These services receive your IP address as part of any normal internet request and handle it under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

LyricWave keeps the following in this app's local storage and IndexedDB on your device. None of it is uploaded to LyricWave servers:

  • Settings: theme, font size, word-by-word reveal, sync offsets, recognition engine, microphone-notice consent.
  • Up to 10 recently identified songs (title, artist, album, artwork link, time).
  • Cached lyrics (kept for up to 30 days to load faster and work offline).
  • Your Last.fm username and Spotify connection, if you use them.
  • Optional LyricWave profile: if you sign up, your display name, email address and a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password are stored only on this device. Profiles do not sync between devices and are not sent anywhere.

5. Analytics & Diagnostics

LyricWave contains no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels or third-party analytics. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address and request time) to deliver and protect the service.

6. Your Choices & Contact

You control all data LyricWave stores:

  • Delete your LyricWave profile: open the account menu and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your name, email and password hash from the device.
  • Erase everything: Settings → Clear All Data removes all preferences, recent songs, cached lyrics, your Last.fm username, Spotify tokens and local profiles. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage does the same.
  • "Reset Defaults" only restores display settings; it does not delete your data.
